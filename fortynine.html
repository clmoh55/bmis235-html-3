49 Degrees North Mountain Resort

Chewelah Peak Glade & Tree-Skiing Heaven

Nestled in the Colville National Forest near Chewelah, WA, 49 Degrees North offers 2,325 acres of wide-open terrain, legendary tree glades, 1,851 ft of vertical drop, and Washington's longest high-speed quad chairlift!
