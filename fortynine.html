49 Degrees North Mountain Resort

49 Degrees North is located in Chewelah, Washington, offering over 2,325 acres of terrain across two peaks.

With wide open cruisers, awesome glades, and 1,850 feet of vertical elevation, 49 Degrees North delivers a top-tier ski experience.
