About 49 Degrees North

49 Degrees North Mountain Resort is located near Chewelah, Washington, in the Colville National Forest. Spanning 2,325 skiable acres across Chewelah Peak and Angel Peak, it offers 1,850 feet of vertical drop and 7 chairlifts, famous for deep tree glades and uncrowded alpine powder.
