Inland Northwest Winter Slopes

The Inland Northwest is home to some of the best skiing and snowboarding in the region. Whether you're looking for deep powder, well-groomed trails, or family-friendly slopes, nearby resorts offer great winter adventures for all skill levels.

Explore three popular winter destinations in the area:

  • Schweitzer Mountain Resort
  • Mt. Spokane Ski & Snowboard Park
  • 49 Degrees North Mountain Resort
