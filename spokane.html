About Mt. Spokane

Mt. Spokane Ski & Snowboard Park is a non-profit ski area located inside Mt. Spokane State Park. Just 28 miles northeast of downtown Spokane, it features 1,708 skiable acres, 6 chairlifts, and extensive night skiing until 9 PM Wednesday through Saturday.
