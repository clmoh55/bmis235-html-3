Mt. Spokane Ski & Snowboard Park

Mt. Spokane is a non-profit ski area located just 28 miles northeast of downtown Spokane, Washington.

Featuring 1,708 acres of skiable terrain, night skiing 5 nights a week, and 6 chairlifts, Mt. Spokane is the local favorite mountain.

Check out Mt. Spokane Official Site for current snow reports.
