Mt. Spokane Ski & Snowboard Park

Spokane's Local Non-Profit Shred Mountain

Just 28 miles northeast of Spokane, Mt. Spokane Ski & Snowboard Park delivers legendary PNW steeze with 1,704 skiable acres, 52 runs, and a vibrant non-profit community spirit. It's the ultimate home mountain for Gonzaga riders!
