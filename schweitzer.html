Schweitzer Mountain Resort

Schweitzer is the largest ski resort in Idaho and Washington, offering 2,900 acres of terrain overlooking Lake Pend Oreille.

Located near Sandpoint, Idaho, Schweitzer boasts a vertical drop of 2,400 feet, breathtaking views, and amazing tree skiing.

Visit the Schweitzer Official Website for trail maps and snow conditions.
