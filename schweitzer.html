Schweitzer Mountain Resort

The King of the Selkirks

Located near Sandpoint, Idaho, Schweitzer boasts 2,900 acres of terrain overlooking Lake Pend Oreille. With 92 trails, 10 lifts, and deep Outback powder stashes, it is an absolute PNW winter paradise!
