About Schweitzer

Schweitzer is the largest ski resort in Idaho and Washington, offering 2,900 acres of terrain, 10 lifts, and 2,400 feet of vertical drop. Located in northern Idaho's Selkirk Mountains, it provides unbeatable tree skiing and stunning vistas of Lake Pend Oreille.
