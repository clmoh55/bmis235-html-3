Schweitzer Mountain Resort

Massive Terrain & Lake Views in Sandpoint, Idaho

Overview

Schweitzer is the largest ski resort in Idaho and Washington, featuring 2,900 acres of terrain, deep snow, and stunning views of Lake Pend Oreille.
